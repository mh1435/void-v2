MOBILE LEGENDS
⚔ ROAMER ROUTES
Standard Route
1Help gold laner at bottom — trade or secure a kill level 1–2
2Move to river → assist jungler clearing turtle camp
3Rotate mid — control LBM / RBM bushes for vision
4Return bottom if enemy dives, or push top if exp needs help
🔴 Gold Lane Is Weak
→Stay bottom longer — 2v2 the enemy or zone them out
→Help gold farm safely under tower with roamer pressure
→Counter-gank when enemy roamer visits bottom
→Avoid forcing 2v1 if you're losing the trade
🔴 Mid Lane Is Weak
→Frequent mid rotations through river — set up kills
→Secure blue buff for your mage when possible
→Camp LBM or RBM to deny enemy jungler's mid ganks
→Zone out enemy mid from turtle vision
🔴 Exp Lane Is Weak
→Visit top lane at level 2–3 before they get dived
→After turtle, immediately rotate top to even the pressure
→Deep ward enemy top jungle to track their jungler
🌀 JUNGLER ROUTES
Standard Clear
1Start at buff camp closest to your gold lane for roamer leash
2Clear second buff camp, then small camps in your jungle
3Look for gank opportunity mid or bottom after clear
4Contest or secure turtle with full team at 3–4 min
Aggressive Invade
1Level 1 invade enemy buff with roamer — steal or force enemy to waste time
2Counter-jungle enemy small camps for gold advantage
3Use gold lead to snowball early ganks
🔴 Gold Lane Is Weak
→After first clear, immediately gank bottom to relieve pressure
→Focus on helping carry snowball rather than counter-jungling
→Secure turtle with gold laner to restore their economy
🔴 Mid Lane Is Weak
→Prioritize mid ganks — easy kills with mid CC setup
→Secure blue buff and leave it for your mage
→Don't let enemy sneak turtle from mid river
🏹 GOLD LANE TIPS
1Trade aggressively level 1 with roamer — hit level 2 first
2Last-hit minions under tower safely when getting dived
3Recall when enemy jungler shows on mini-map bottom
4Rotate for turtle with full skills ready — your damage matters
5Split push side lane when team holds a fight elsewhere
✨ MID LANE TIPS
1Fast wave clear → immediately rotate to turtle
2Control LBM (Left Bush Mid) and RBM for vision advantage
3First to arrive at turtle = biggest influence on early game
4Track enemy jungler position from mini-map — call it out
5After turtle, push mid tower before rotating elsewhere
🛡 EXP LANE TIPS
1Win the 1v1 or play safe under tower — don't feed
2Never let opponent hit level 4 before you — deny their skill advantage
3Rotate when opponent recalls — push wave into tower first
4Join teamfight as frontline or secondary carry
5After lord spawns, teleport/rotate to teamfights — don't solo top
📍 MAP CALLOUTS
LBMLeft Bush Mid — bush on the left side of mid lane, used for ambushes and vision control
RBMRight Bush Mid — same as LBM but right side. Always check before walking mid
TurtleFirst major river objective. Spawns at ~1:30. Grants gold + exp bonus to team
LordLate-game objective that pushes a lane with minions. Spawns at 10:00+
Blue BuffJungle camp near mid — reduces mana cost and cooldowns. Priority for mage/jungler
Red BuffJungle camp near gold lane — adds slow to basic attacks. Good for junglers/fighters
RiverCentral water area connecting top/mid/bottom. Key rotation path for roamers
High GroundElevated areas near base giving vision advantage. Defend here in late game
🧠 MACRO & MICRO
MacroBig-picture strategy: objective control, rotation timing, wave management, knowing when to fight
MicroIndividual mechanics: hitting skill shots, combos, kiting, positioning in fights
RotationMoving from your lane to another to help teammates or contest objectives
VisionMap awareness — knowing where enemies are. Lack of vision = don't overextend
Wave ClearKilling minions quickly to free yourself to rotate
SnowballUsing an early lead to grow your advantage — kills → items → more kills
BackdoorSneaking to push towers while enemies are distracted elsewhere
Split PushOne hero pushes a side lane alone while team fights or defends
PokeDealing safe ranged damage to weaken enemies before committing to a fight
DiveAttacking enemies who are hiding under their own tower — risky, high reward
ZoneControlling an area so enemies can't enter or farm
StackBuilding up passive charges (e.g. Aldous stacks, Lesley stacks) by farming
⚡ COMBAT TERMS
CCCrowd Control — any skill that limits enemy movement or actions
Hard CCStrong CC: stun, suppress, knock-up, knock-back. Stops the enemy completely
Soft CCWeaker CC: slow, root (can't move but can use skills)
SuppressStrongest CC — enemy can't move, use skills, or blink. Kaja and Franco ult
BurstDealing huge damage in a very short window — assassin playstyle
SustainHealing or staying alive over a long fight — fighters, some mages
PeelProtecting your carry from enemy assassins — body-blocking, CCing divers
KiteAttacking while moving away — keeps you safe while dealing damage
AOEArea of Effect — skill that hits multiple enemies in a radius
OutplayUsing mechanics or positioning to beat an enemy who should have won
ResetSkill cooldown refreshes after a kill — lets you chain more kills (Karina, Saber)
🎭 ROLES & POSITIONS
GoldGold laner — Marksman in bottom lane. Priority: farm → late-game DPS carry
EXPExp laner — Fighter/tank in top lane. Priority: win 1v1, be frontline in teamfights
MidMid laner — Mage in center lane. Priority: wave clear, rotation, burst damage
Jungle / JGLJungler — Farms jungle camps, ganks lanes, controls objectives. Uses Retribution spell
RoamRoamer — Support/tank who roams to assist all lanes. Uses Encourage/Dire Hit/Conceal
Hyper CarryJungler who farms heavily to become very powerful late game (Yi Sun-shin, Claude, etc.)
CoreMain carry of the team — usually the jungler. Needs most farm and kills
OfflanerExp laner who can handle pressure alone — Fighters like Chou, Paquito, Badang
FrontlineTanks/fighters who absorb damage at the front of team fights
BacklineSquishies (mage/marksman) who deal damage from a safe distance behind tanks
